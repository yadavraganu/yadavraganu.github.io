Landing Pad – Customer Name Screening

Client: Major European Bank  ·  Role: ETL Developer  ·  Team Size: 8  ·  Duration: Feb 2015 – Jul 2016

Designed, built, and maintained a global customer name-screening platform for a major European bank. The system screened the bank’s worldwide customer base against sanctions and watchlists to identify potential involvement in illegal activity. I worked across the complete ETL lifecycle, collecting regional branch data, transforming it, and loading it into Oracle in a structured, screening-ready format.

Key Contributions:

  • Analyzed mapping specifications and data requirement documents and recommended design improvements.
  • Developed parallel ETL and sequence jobs using DataStage stages including CDC, Transformer, Join, Lookup, Oracle Connector, and partitioning strategies.
  • Processed incremental delta loads and tuned jobs for throughput, performance, and resource efficiency.
  • Ran and monitored jobs through DataStage Director and Unix, investigated failures using job logs, and validated data using SQL.
  • Supported unit and integration testing, production deployments, database components, Unix scripts, and Control-M scheduling.
